# Get notified about new PRs in a repo

1. Open the repository menu above the PR list. It says **All repos** until you pick one.
2. Click the **bell** next to the repository.

From now on, a new PR in that repository shows in the bell in the title bar, and as a Windows notification. Click it to open the PR.

To be told about drafts as soon as they're opened (rather than when they're published), turn on **Include new drafts** in Settings → Notifications.

## Have them reviewed as they arrive

Turn on **Automatically review new PRs with your default reviewer** in Settings → Notifications (it needs **New pull requests in watched repositories** on). New PRs in your watched repositories are reviewed in the background, and the notification says when the findings are ready. Nothing is posted: you go through them as usual.

## Stop watching

Click the bell in the repository menu again, or the **×** next to the repository in Settings → Notifications.

Watched repositories are remembered per project: switching projects keeps them for when you come back.

To keep notifications coming with the window closed, leave **Keep AzTech PRador running in the system tray when the window is closed** on, near the bottom of Settings. See [Notifications](../notifications.md).
